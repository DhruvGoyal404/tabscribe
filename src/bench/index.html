Per-call cost

With the loaded model: one English decode of a 2, 5, 10 and 15 s segment, each taken from three places in the English clip after a warm-up call. Feature extraction, encoder and decoder are timed separately. Medians are shown.

Threads is ONNX Runtime's WASM thread count (it also runs the few CPU ops on WebGPU). It's fixed when a worker first loads a model, so changing it restarts the worker and reloads the model from cache before measuring. Load model uses it too.
